Hacker Pen Privacy Policy
Last updated: September 26, 2026

Overview

Hacker Pen is an independent reader for public Hacker News content. You do not need a Hacker Pen account. We do not operate a backend that receives or stores your reading activity, API keys, or AI requests. The app does not include developer-operated advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services.

Information stored on your device

Your AI provider, selected model, translation language, translation display, and reading preferences are saved locally. API keys are stored using the operating system's secure storage, including Keychain on iOS. Keys are sent to their respective providers to authenticate requests; they are not sent to the Hacker Pen developer or other AI providers.

Fetched Hacker News content and AI results are cached in memory to avoid repeated requests. These app caches are not a developer-hosted database or a permanent reading history. Embedded websites may separately store cookies and website data on your device. Device backups and secure-storage persistence are controlled by your operating system and its settings.

Hacker News and linked websites

The app connects directly to the Hacker News API, hosted by Firebase, to load public stories, comments, and profiles. Opening an article connects to the article's website. These services receive the requested address and network information such as your IP address. Linked websites may use cookies, analytics, advertisements, and other technologies under their own privacy policies. The Hacker Pen developer does not receive a copy of these requests.

Optional AI features

AI summaries and translations require a key for the provider you choose: OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google Gemini. Reading Hacker News does not require an AI key.

When you configure a provider or choose a model, the app may contact that provider to retrieve available models. When you request a summary, the app fetches the article and sends its URL, extracted text, and requested output language directly to the selected AI provider. Translation sends the selected comment text, requested language, and translation instructions. Translating replies sends the text of the replies in that thread. Content may include names, links, or other information contained in the original article or comments.

AI requests use your provider account and may incur charges from that provider. The provider receives your API credentials, submitted content, and network information. Its applicable API terms, privacy policy, and account settings govern processing, retention, and any use for model training. Hacker Pen does not control or promise deletion of data retained by those providers.

Provider privacy information:
OpenAI: https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/
Anthropic: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy
Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Your choices and deletion

AI features are optional. To prevent future AI requests from Hacker Pen using a saved key, open Settings > Providers & keys, select the provider, and choose Remove API key. Removing a key does not cancel requests already sent or delete data already held by a provider. You can also revoke the key through the provider's account controls and contact that provider about its data-retention or deletion options.

You can change local preferences in Settings. In-memory caches do not survive termination of the app process. Removing the app removes ordinary app-local data, subject to operating-system backup and restore behavior. Secure-storage entries may survive uninstall, so remove saved keys in the app before uninstalling or revoke them with the provider.

There is no Hacker Pen account or developer-hosted reading history to delete. Questions about data held by Hacker News, a linked website, or an AI provider should be directed to that service.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions, use the developer support contact on Hacker Pen's App Store product page. Do not include API keys in support messages. If you contact us, we use the information you provide to address your request.

We will update this policy when the app's data practices change. The date above identifies this version.
